Complete Trading Guidance

Beginner se disciplined trader banne tak ka practical roadmap.

1. Market Basics

Forex, crypto, stocks aur indices ka basic structure samjhein. Price, spread, leverage, liquidity aur volatility ko samajhna zaroori hai.

2. Technical Analysis

Trend, support/resistance, market structure, candlesticks aur volume ko combine karke entries aur exits identify karein.

3. Risk Management

Har trade par limited risk rakhein. Stop-loss use karein aur position size ko account ke risk ke mutabiq calculate karein.

4. Trading Psychology

FOMO, revenge trading, greed aur overtrading se bachna seekhein. Plan ke baghair trade na karein.

5. Trading Plan

Entry condition, stop-loss, take-profit, risk percentage aur invalidation point pehle define karein.

6. Trading Journal

Har trade ka reason, setup, result aur emotional state record karein. Weekly review se mistakes identify karein.
